Tests today are broken

Real knowledge stays hidden

When every student gets the same paper, you can't tell who actually understood. Grades stop showing what each one really knows.

Hours of grading

28 papers × 5 problems × cross-checking. Evenings and weekends disappear on what could be automated.

Same problems every year

Last year's answer key is already on TikTok. Rewriting the problem set from scratch — another wasted evening.
